Melisa is an intern in the Velten Lab and a Master's student in Molecular Biotechnology at Heidelberg University. She obtained her B.Sc. in Molecular Biotechnology from Turkish-German University in Istanbul. During her internship, she investigates how genetic perturbations affect gene expression over time using single-cell transcriptomic data and factor models.

In her free time, Melisa enjoys drawing, reading, traveling, and learning new languages.
