Our paper ["Comparison of interventional causal structure learning algorithms for gene regulatory network inference"](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s12859-026-06728-1) is now published in BMC Bioinformatics!
<!-- excerpt end -->

Jan Sprengel's new benchmark asks whether causal structure learning algorithms can actually use the information revealed by targeted interventions to infer gene regulatory networks.
The algorithms are evaluated on synthetic single-cell perturbation data and on experimental data from a CRISPR screen, using metrics tailored to synthetic ground truth and to real biological data respectively.
You can find the full paper [HERE](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s12859-026-06728-1).
