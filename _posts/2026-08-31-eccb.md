Jana, Jan and Zehua were at [ECCB 2026](https://eccb2026.org)!
<!-- excerpt end -->
The 25th European Conference on Computational Biology took place in Geneva from 31 August to 4 September, and our team was there with three posters:

- Jana presented "Joint inference of guide assignment and perturbation effects in heterogeneous single-cell CRISPR screens".
- Jan presented "Benchmarking Interventional Causal Structure Learning for Gene Regulatory Network Inference".
- Zehua presented "DRAFT: Dynamic Gene Regulatory Network Inference with Graph-Recurrent Attention from Single-cell Multi-Omics Trajectories".

It was a great experience for them: presenting their work, engaging with many researchers, exchanging ideas, and receiving so much valuable feedback and so many suggestions.

Outside the conference, they had a wonderful time exploring Geneva and Lausanne together.
