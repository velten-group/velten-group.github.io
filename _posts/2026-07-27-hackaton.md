Some teams fuel their hackathons with energy drinks. We started ours with pretzels and banana bread — because if you are going to spend a whole day rethinking how you work, you should at least do it well fed. 
<!-- excerpt end -->
Everyone shared their workflows, their favourite solutions, but also their pain points: nothing is quite as honest as a room full of colleagues describing the tasks that make them sigh.
And part of the hackathon output? The very blog you are reading right now, and more still to come...
